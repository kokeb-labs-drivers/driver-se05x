SE052 guide¶

The SE052F is a different package with a different pin table: no ENA pin, SDA and SCL on other pins, a VDD that accepts up to 5.5 V. Do not reuse an SE050 footprint.

Datasheet sections this page relies on
  • Pin table of the HVQFN20 package (SE052): NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 2 Pin description, p. 4
  • SE052 package HVQFN20 (SOT917-7): NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, 6 Package, p. 5
  • SE052 system integration: pull-ups, VIN and VOUT capacitors: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Figure 1 SE052 using I2C, p. 6
  • SE052 power-down by RELEASE and deep power-down by S-block 0xDF: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, 10 Power-saving modes, p. 7
  • SE052 ATR bytes: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 12 Configuration parameters, p. 16
  • SE052 CIP bytes in the configuration: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 12 Configuration parameters, p. 17
  • SE052 supplies: VDD 1.62 V to 5.5 V, VIN 1.65 V to 3.6 V: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 13 IC supply current, p. 18
  • SE052 I2C parameters, flavour and address in the configuration: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 12 I2C_SPI_PARAMS, I2C_SLAVE_ADDRESS, p. 18
  • SE052 RST_N reset pulse width: NXP SE052 Rev. 1.5, Table 14 AC characteristics, p. 20
  • NXP flavour ATR: NXP UM11225 Rev. 1.2, Tables 11 to 13, p. 9
  • Limits of FIPS 140-3 mode: NXP AN12543 Rev. 4.5, 3.13.2 FIPS 140-3 mode, p. 45
  • SE052F configuration: applet 7.2.22, AppletConfig 0x26F2, OEF B501: NXP AN14277 Rev. 1.0, SE052F configuration, p. 2
  • WTX host timeout at least 4 s; NXP mode is the default: NXP AN13904 Rev. 1.0, WTX and T=1 mode, p. 27
  • APDU throughput limit on the SE052F: NXP AN13904 Rev. 1.0, APDU throughput limit, p. 28

All of them, with the driver API for each: Datasheet map.

Pinout¶

Figure description and sources

The SE052 seen from the top in its HVQFN20 package (4 x 4 x 0.85 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, SOT917-7 in the data sheet). Pin 1 is at the top of the left side and the numbers run counter-clockwise: 1 to 5 down the left side, 6 to 10 along the bottom, 11 to 15 up the right side and 16 to 20 along the top. Each pin card is coloured by the pin's type as in the HX2QFN20 pinout. The driver uses SDA (11) and SCL (10) for the bus and RST_N (14) through the reset port; the SE052 has no ENA pin, so deep power-down is a T=1 S-block (0xDF) and the next I2C access wakes it. The table is SE052 Table 2 (p. 4).

Drawn from: docs/diagrams/figures/_se052.py; figure module docs/diagrams/figures/se052-pinout.py.

Figure description and sources

The SE052 body from above and from the side, to one scale: 4.0 x 4.0 mm, 0.85 mm high, 20 pins at a 0.5 mm pitch, five on each side, pin 1 at the top of the left side (SE052 data sheet section 6, p. 5, and the pin order of Table 2, p. 4). The terminal and exposed-pad dimensions are in the package drawing SOT917-7, not in the data sheet, so the pins are drawn as position marks only. The SE050 and SE051 come in the smaller HX2QFN20 (3.0 x 3.0 x 0.32 mm, 0.4 mm pitch).

Drawn from: docs/diagrams/figures/_se052.py; figure module docs/diagrams/figures/se052-outline.py.

HVQFN20, 4 x 4 x 0.85 mm, 0.5 mm pitch. The data sheet names the outline SOT917-7 (section 6, p. 5) and AN14028 names SOT917-6: check the package data sheet before the footprint (ambiguity A2).

Wiring¶

Figure description and sources

The SE052 on an MCU's I2C bus. SDA (pin 11) and SCL (pin 10) go to the MCU with pull-ups to the bus supply of 1.65 V to 3.6 V, which also feeds VIN (pin 12). VOUT (pin 15) goes to VDD (pin 18) on the board, as Table 2 asks; Figure 1 shows an optional 22 nF on VIN and an optional 22 nF (or nothing) on VOUT. RST_N (pin 14) goes to a GPIO the reset port drives low to reset the chip. There is no ENA pin: deep power-down is the S-block 0xDF, and the next I2C access wakes the chip. CLK (13) is left open or tied to ground, LA and LB are left open, IO1 and IO2 are the chip's own I2C controller and stay open when it is not used. The pull-up value is not in the data sheet.

Drawn from: docs/diagrams/figures/_se052.py; figure module docs/diagrams/figures/se052-wiring.py.

  • Supplies. VIN 1.65 V to 3.6 V feeds the I2C pads; VDD takes 1.62 V to 5.5 V in three classes (Table 13, p. 18). VOUT is wired to VDD on the board (Table 2), so deep power-down can switch the core off.
  • RST_N low resets the chip (Table 2); a pulse of at least 40 us (Table 14, p. 20). The reset port drives it; without one the ladder ends at the chip-reset S-block.
  • No ENA. Deep power-down is the T=1 S-block 0xDF; the next I2C access to the address wakes and boots the chip, with nothing kept (section 10.3, p. 7).
  • Pull-ups to the bus supply (Figure 1, p. 6); the data sheet gives no value.

Configuration¶

The SE052F runs applet 7.2.22 with AppletConfig 0x26F2 and OEF B501 (AN14277 p. 2). Its configuration table (Table 12, pp. 17-18) sets the I2C address 0x48, clock stretching off (up to 1 MHz), power saving after the end of an APDU session, and lists the CIP bytes: BWT 1000 ms, IFSC 254, MPOT 1 ms, power wake-up 5 ms. The same table names the GP 0.39 flavour while AN13904 p. 27 calls the NXP mode the default; TransportFlavour::Auto settles it on the wire. The FIPS 140-3 mode leaves out EdDSA, Montgomery curves, DES, MIFARE DESFire and PAKE (AN12543 3.13.2, p. 45); the capability gate refuses them before sending.

Throughput limit¶

More than one million APDUs within 34 days and the SE052F answers SW 0x66A6 to everything until it is reset (AN13904 p. 28). The driver resets the chip once (ThroughputLimited, then Resynced) and counts APDUs in ApduBudget; a design that talks to the chip once per second exceeds the limit in under two weeks.

Self-test¶

In FIPS mode, run the self-tests from the default session before AES-GCM, GMAC, PBKDF2 and the TLS KDF (AN13030 p. 817). TriggerSelfTest may reset the chip with no reply; the driver asks for ResetExpected.

Bench status: not verified on hardware.
