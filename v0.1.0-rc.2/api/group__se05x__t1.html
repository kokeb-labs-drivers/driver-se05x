◆ ResetKind

enum class se05x::t1::ResetKind : uint8_t
strong

How Link::Reset() resets the chip.

Reset and PowerCycle call the reset port; what the port drives is the board's choice. On the SE050 and SE051 RST_N is the ISO 7816 reset and has no effect when only I2C is enabled (SE050 data sheet 4.2 p. 9, SE051 p. 10): there Reset() must power-cycle through ENA (or the supply), or the GP-flavour chip-reset rung, which uses it, resets nothing. On the SE052 RST_N resets the chip (SE052 Table 14 p. 20, at least 40 us low).

Enumerator
Interface 

S-block interface soft reset: the chip keeps its state.

Cold 

The NXP chip reset S-block (0xC6, UM11225); Unsupported in the GP flavour, which defines no cold reset (GPC_SPE_172).

Reset 

The reset port's Reset(): not RST_N on an I2C-only SE050/SE051 (see above).

PowerCycle 

The reset port's PowerCycle(): supply or ENA off and on.
