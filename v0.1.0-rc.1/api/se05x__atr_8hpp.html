Detailed Description

The link parameters from the ATR (UM11225 flavour) and the CIP (GP flavour).

Copyright
Copyright (c) 2026 The driver-se05x authors. SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT

Frozen surface: LinkParams, ParseAtr(), ParseCip(). The ATR and CIP replace the defaults of kDefaultIfs and kDefaultBwtMs at Link::Open(). Both parsers are strict: every length field must fit, the structure must end exactly at the last byte, and a value the link cannot use is refused with FrameError. They are fuzz targets (fuzz_atr_cip).

ATR (UM11225 section 2.2, Tables 11 to 13, pp. 9-10): PVER (1), VID (5), DLLP length (1), DLLP (BWT 2 bytes in ms, IFSC 2 bytes, extra bytes ignored), PLID (1, always 2 for I2C), PLP length (1), PLP (MCF 2 bytes in kHz, configuration 1 with bit 4 = high-speed mode, MPOT 1 in ms, RFU 1, RFU 2, SEGT 2 in us, WUT 2 in us; extra bytes ignored), HB length (1), HB. Multi-byte values are big endian, as in the SE052 ATR of the SE052 data sheet (Table 12, p. 16). The ATR log of AN13904 pp. 4-5 (vector V8) has the same bytes; it comes from a chip reporting OEF A8FA, an SE051C2 (AN12973 Table 4, p. 4), not an SE052F.

CIP (GPC_SPE_172 section 4.3, Tables 4-6, 4-7 and 4-9, pp. 36-38): PVER (1), IIN length (1), IIN (3 or 4 bytes, BCD), PLID (1, 2 = I2C), PLP length (1), PLP (configuration 1 RFU, PWT 1 in ms, MCF 2 in kHz, PST 1 in ms, MPOT 1 in units of 100 us, RWGT 2 in us; extra bytes ignored), DLLP length (1), DLLP (BWT 2 in ms, IFSC 2; extra bytes ignored), HB length (1, at most 32), HB; at most 64 bytes in all.

Parse an ATR (UM11225 flavour).

Parameters
atrThe ATR bytes as received (the INF of the soft-reset or get-ATR response).
Returns
The parameters, with ifsd equal to ifsc (UM11225 section 2.1.2.1, p. 8), or FrameError: a length that does not fit, bytes left over, PVER 0, a PLID other than 2, a BWT or IFSC of 0, an IFSC above 0xFE, or an MPOT above 65 ms.
