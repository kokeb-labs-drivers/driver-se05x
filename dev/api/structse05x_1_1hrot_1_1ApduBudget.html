Detailed Description

The APDU budget (S-17): the chip may refuse every command with SW 0x66A6 after more than one million APDUs within 34 days (AN14028 2.3.1; SE052F, possible on SE051) until it is reset. The facade counts every APDU the device sends and refuses a call that would pass the ceiling. The recovery after a 0x66A6 answer may add up to four APDUs past the admitted cost (see the file comment); they are counted too.
