Detailed Description

EcKeySessionInternalAuthenticate: 84 88 00 00 <Lc> <A6 ... 7F49 ... 5F37 ...> 00 (AN12543 Tables 85, 86). host_data is the whole payload, built by the ECKey layer; the middleware sends it without an outer TAG_1. The answer 85 10 <challenge> 86 10 <receipt> is checked and kept raw in the reply.
