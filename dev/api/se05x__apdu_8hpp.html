Detailed Description

The APDU layer core: TLV writer and reader, response splitting and status word mapping.

The public surface below is frozen; the helpers in se05x::apdu::detail, which the codecs use, are not part of the contract. The per-command codecs (Codec<C>) are in the group headers se05x_apdu_*.hpp, included by se05x_apdu_codec.hpp.

Wire facts (AN12543 section 4.1, AN12413 section 4.1): CLA 0x80 (0x84 with secure messaging), INS = group code or'ed with the characteristics 0x80 (transient), 0x40 (auth object), 0x20 (attest); a body of TLVs with one-byte tags and ISO 7816-4 BER lengths (one byte below 0x80, 81 xx, or 82 xx xx; a response may use the three-byte form even for short values); a response of TLVs and a two-byte status word. The framing of a command (ISO 7816-4 cases 1 to 4, short or extended) is decided once in detail::CommandWriter::Finish().
